The committee has deferred funding for the staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers, citing significant risks and uncertainties that outweigh potential benefits. Security reviews have raised unresolved prompt-injection concerns, especially in systems handling regulated customer data, which could expose the organization to compliance and reputational risks (ev-015). Additionally, a lower-than-industry-average uptake due to weaker onboarding support per engineer suggests challenges in scaling effectively across diverse codebases and teams (ev-017). Given these factors and the need for further investigation into long-term code quality and security implications, deferring funding until a more robust risk assessment can be developed is deemed prudent.
