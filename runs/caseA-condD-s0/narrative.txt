The committee has deferred funding for a staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to unresolved concerns primarily raised by the CISO regarding potential security risks from open prompt-injection vulnerabilities and the lack of evidence on how the agent performs in systems handling regulated customer data. The decision hinges on further clarification about the real-world incident rate for these vulnerabilities at scale and whether the initial pilot's success metrics can be reliably extrapolated to encompass all enterprise environments, including legacy codebases.
