The committee has deferred Northstar Software Group's investment in an Internal Developer Platform, citing low confidence due to unresolved questions regarding whether toolchain fragmentation is a binding constraint on productivity. Key uncertainties include the total cost of ownership over five years, the opportunity cost of allocating GBP3m-GBP5m and approximately 20 engineers, and the measurable impact of golden paths on reducing security and operational policy exceptions. The strongest dissent emphasizes that without clear evidence addressing these concerns and providing a detailed comparison between incremental improvements and full platform development, the decision to proceed with such a significant investment would be premature and potentially risky.
