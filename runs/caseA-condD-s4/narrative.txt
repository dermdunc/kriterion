The committee has deferred the decision to fund a staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to several critical concerns requiring further investigation. Key issues include an unresolved prompt-injection vulnerability and uncertainty regarding the security review's applicability to systems handling regulated customer data. Additionally, the real-world incident rate for low-frequency security events at full rollout scale remains unknown, posing significant risks. Until these uncertainties are addressed and a robust human-review process is established for AI-suggested changes in regulated environments, funding this proposal would expose the enterprise to unacceptable risk levels.
