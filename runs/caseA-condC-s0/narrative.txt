The committee has decided to defer funding for the staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to significant risks and uncertainties. The security review highlighted unresolved concerns regarding prompt injection in systems handling regulated customer data, posing potential regulatory compliance issues. Moreover, the projected adoption rate of 40-55% within six months falls below industry standards, indicating that without enhanced onboarding support, the full-scale rollout may not yield anticipated productivity improvements and could result in a poor return on investment. Given these factors and the variability in adoption rates across different codebases, further evidence is required to address these critical gaps before proceeding with funding.
