The committee has deferred funding for the staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to unresolved security concerns and uncertainties regarding its performance on legacy codebases and regulated-data systems. Key issues include the unmitigated risk of open prompt-injection vulnerabilities (ev-008) at full scale and the lack of a documented process for human review of AI-suggested changes in regulated environments (ev-012). Additionally, the pilot did not cover any system handling regulated customer data (ev-015), leaving significant doubts about its readiness for enterprise-wide deployment. Given these conditions, the potential risks currently outweigh the benefits, leading to a medium confidence decision to defer further action until these critical questions are adequately addressed.
