The proposed staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers has been deferred due to significant uncertainties and risks that currently outweigh its potential benefits. The pilot phase did not adequately address security concerns for systems handling regulated customer data, raising fears of unforeseen vulnerabilities in a full-scale deployment. Moreover, the projected adoption rate is below industry norms owing to insufficient onboarding support, casting doubt on the anticipated usage levels and associated benefits. Given these challenges and the high financial risk indicated by a wide NPV range (GBP-5,351,240 / GBP5,085,455 / GBP38,138,347), further validation is necessary before proceeding with funding.
