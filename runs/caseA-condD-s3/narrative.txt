The committee has deferred funding for a staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to low confidence in several critical areas. Key concerns include unresolved security vulnerabilities related to prompt-injection and inadequate testing on systems handling regulated customer data, which raises questions about the generalizability of pilot results. Additionally, there is uncertainty regarding the real-world incident rate for these vulnerabilities at full scale and a lack of documented processes aligning with regulatory guidance for human review of AI-suggested changes in regulated systems. These unresolved issues necessitate further investigation before proceeding with enterprise-wide deployment.
