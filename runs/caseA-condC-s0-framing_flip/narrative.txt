As committee chair, I summarize that we have deferred funding for the staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to medium confidence in several key conditions: whether the security review adequately covers regulated-data systems, the generalizability of pilot benefits across the broader engineering population, and concerns over full-scale adoption rates given legacy codebase challenges. While a business executive strongly advocated for proceeding with a pilot based on demonstrated acceptance and cycle-time reduction, we remain cautious about justifying a full rollout without further exploration into these uncertainties.
