The committee has deferred funding for a staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to unresolved concerns regarding the security review's adequacy for systems handling regulated data and the potential risk posed by open prompt-injection vulnerabilities (ev-008). The pilot phase did not include testing with regulated customer data (ev-015), leaving questions about the generalizability of current findings. Additionally, the real-world incident rate for low-frequency security events at full rollout scale remains unknown (ev-026), further complicating risk assessment and decision-making.
