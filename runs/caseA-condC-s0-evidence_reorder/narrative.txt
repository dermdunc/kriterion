The committee has deferred funding for a staged rollout of an enterprise coding agent to 5,000 engineers due to significant uncertainties surrounding security implications, particularly in systems handling regulated customer data, and unresolved prompt-injection concerns. There is also no established methodology to attribute observed cycle-time or defect-rate changes specifically to the agent's usage at scale, complicating efforts to quantify economic benefits accurately. Given these challenges and the reliance on several low-strength assumptions in the economics model, further validation through targeted reviews and detailed feasibility studies is deemed necessary before proceeding with a broader rollout.
